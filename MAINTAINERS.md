# Maintainers

AgentIsOK is in initial stewardship. Named individual maintainers will be published at public launch. Until then, stewardship rests with Univeracity through this repository.

| Role | Who | Contact |
|---|---|---|
| Steward | Univeracity | GitHub organization `Univeracity` |
| Security | Maintainers | Private vulnerability reporting when the repository is public; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) |

Stewardship does not grant unilateral ownership of the protocol. No permanent normative role is reserved for Univeracity or an AgentIsOK-operated service. See [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

Protocol and governance changes follow the review process in GOVERNANCE.md. Once two or more named maintainers exist, substantive protocol changes require approval from at least two of them.
